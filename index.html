Workflows & Operating Procedures

Standard procedures and checklists for OICL operations

Motor Claim Process

  • Customer reports claim with policy number & incident details
  • Download Motor Claim Form from Claims tab
  • Fill form with claim details (date, location, damage)
  • Attach supporting documents (FIR, photos, estimate)
  • Submit to OICL Claims office / surveyor
  • Track claim status on OICL portal

Fire Claim Process

  • Document all damages immediately & notify OICL officer
  • Download Fire Claim Form from Claims tab
  • Collect proof of ownership / inventory list
  • Get surveyor inspection report
  • Submit form with supporting financial documents
  • OICL will process and settle claim

Proposal Underwriting Process

  • Customer fills appropriate Proposal Form (Forms tab)
  • Provide accurate information about risk parameters
  • Attach supporting documents (KYC, address proof, photos)
  • OICL AO reviews proposal & calculates premium
  • Policy issued upon approval and premium receipt
